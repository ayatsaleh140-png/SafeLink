What is SafeLink?

SafeLink is a website that helps users determine whether a website, link, or email may be unsafe.

Users can enter a URL, domain name, or email text, and SafeLink analyzes it for signs of scams, phishing attempts, and other online threats.

The Problem

Many people receive suspicious emails, text messages, and website links every day.

It can be difficult to tell whether a website is legitimate or a scam. As a result, people may lose money, personal information, or access to their accounts.

There needs to be a simple way for everyday users to check whether a link is safe before clicking on it.

Our Solution

SafeLink helps users identify dangerous websites and emails by:

Comparing websites against a database of known scam domains
Using AI to analyze suspicious language
Warning users about potential phishing attempts
Providing a simple safety assessment

Challenges We Faced

One challenge was trying to make the AI directly visit and analyze websites in real time.

Because this was difficult to implement during development, we created a large database of known scam domains that can be updated over time.

We also used AI to detect suspicious language commonly found in scam emails and phishing messages.

This allowed us to build a reliable solution despite technical limitations.
